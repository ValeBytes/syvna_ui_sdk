# FeatureRows

The product shown a part at a time: words on one side and a large real screen on the other, swapping sides down the page. The strongest section a product's landing page can have, because it shows the thing.

- Two to four rows, one per part of the product a buyer cares about, in the order they'd use them.
- `media` is a real screen of the app (built in HTML with real data, or a screenshot the person gave), large. Never an icon or an illustration.
- The title says what the person can do ("Approve from your phone"), not the feature's name. `points` are two or three short reasons.
- `animate` brings words and picture in from their own sides.
