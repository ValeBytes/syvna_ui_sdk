# Hero

The top of a landing page. In one look: what it is (title), who it's for and what changes (description), and the one thing to do next (actions). 21 variants take the same content, so trying another is one prop.

- The title is a plain claim a visitor understands, not a slogan. Keep it under ten words.
- One primary action. A second, if any, is secondary ("See how it works").
- `note` removes the doubt next to the button: "Free for 14 days. No card needed."

Which variant:

- **Software with a screenshot:** `center`, `browser` (the screenshot in a window), `tilted` (tilted back, fading out), `grid` or `glow` (a soft background).
- **Text beside a picture:** `split`, `split-reverse`; `stats` adds three or four proving numbers (`stats`).
- **Brands, shops, places:** `photo` or `photo-bottom` over a full-bleed photo (`image`); `collage` for several products (`gallery`).
- **Words first:** `minimal` (the title alone), `editorial` (a large title with the words beside it), `left`.
- **A band that stands out:** `inverted` (on ink), `brand` (on the brand colour), `card`, `gradient`, `bordered`.
- **Launches and waitlists:** `announcement` (a "New" pill on the eyebrow), `signup` (an email field as the action, `signup`).
